**Source:** DataForSEO hub-seed pulls, es-MX (loc 2484) + es-ES (loc 2724), language `es`,
15 SERP-modeled seeds each. Cost $0.47 total. Files:
`keyword-data/ghl-es-hubs-mx-20260624.json` + `…-es-20260624.json` (+ reports).
Demand-shape context: [[ghl-trial-keyword-research-multilang-2026-06]] (trial is ~10/mo;
real intent is precios / "es gratis" / qué es).

## Design rules (Caleb method, same as the EN map)
- Each hub = 1 **rankable GHL-branded head** + ~15 spokes. Brand terms are low-volume but
  low-competition + high commercial intent (we can win them). Generic category terms
  (whatsapp business api, embudos de ventas, automatización de marketing) are higher-volume
  TOFU that feed topical authority and capture researchers.
- Spokes blend: PAA questions (verbatim), related searches, branded long-tail, competitor-vs terms.
- **LIVE** = post already published; **DRAFT** = post file exists, not deployed; rest = NEW.

## Volume signals that shaped this (MX / ES)
- `whatsapp business api` **1900 / 1300** (KD 17–18) — biggest non-brand ES term by far.
- `gohighlevel` (brand) **9,900** (KD 5–9) — the head; navigational/informational.
- `embudos de ventas` **320 / 260** (KD 7–20) — strong + low difficulty.
- `automatización de marketing` **170 / 210** (KD 0–23).
- `gohighlevel login` **1000** — navigational, big.
- `gohighlevel precios` / `que es` / `opiniones` — 20–50 each but PAA-rich, high intent.
- ⚠️ `recepcionista virtual` 40–50 but **KD 40–50 and SERP = job listings** (Indeed/Computrabajo/
  Bumeran). In Spanish this means the *job*, not AI software → AI-receptionist is a WEAK ES hub
  (it's #1 in English). Folded into Automation as a couple of spokes, not its own hub.

## Honest read for Bill
- 8 core hubs are solidly demand-backed; H9 (Pagos) makes 9 if we want the LATAM payments lever.
- Hubs **4 (WhatsApp), 6 (Embudos), 5 (Automatización)** carry the real generic volume.
- Hubs **2 (Precios), 3 (Opiniones), 1 (Qué es)** are low-volume but highest-conversion + rankable.
- Spanish has nowhere near English's depth — many spokes are sub-50/mo long-tail. That's correct
  for topical authority but won't individually move traffic; the heads + WhatsApp do the lifting.
