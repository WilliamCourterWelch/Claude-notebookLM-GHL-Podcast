Do we “force” people through the hubs? For SEO, yes — the crawl/authority path is home → hub → spoke. For people it's actually better: clicking “For service businesses” lands them on a rich, complete hub with every relevant guide, instead of a random 6-tile grid. The only shortcut we keep is the trial guide, because that page is the one making the money.

Why this wins (link juice, in plain terms)

Concentration. A page passes a fixed amount of authority, split across its links. 9 links → thin slices. 3 links (2 hubs + money page) → each hub gets a big share and ranks harder for its head term (“gohighlevel agency”, “gohighlevel for service business”).
Silo integrity. Topics stay separated. Google sees “this site has two clear themes, each deep,” not “a homepage that links to 30 unrelated pages.” That clarity is part of what you lost in the demotion.
Crawl efficiency. A clean tree (home → hub → spoke) gets crawled and re-crawled predictably. New spokes you add under a hub get found fast because the path to them is obvious.
