The basics

What is a "GoHighLevel agency"?

A GoHighLevel agency is a marketing or service business that uses one GoHighLevel account to run many clients — and, in most cases, resells the platform to those clients under its own brand. Instead of paying for a separate CRM, email tool, funnel builder, and scheduler for every client, the agency manages all of them from a single dashboard, then charges each client a monthly fee to use the branded version.

Two things make this work: sub-accounts (a walled-off workspace per client) and white-labelling (your logo and domain on the whole thing). Together they turn GoHighLevel from "a tool you use" into "a SaaS product you sell."

The core idea

What white-label actually means

White-label isn't a login screen with your name on it. It means the entire platform carries your brand: your domain, your logo, your colors, your mobile app name. Your client signs in at app.youragency.com, sees your branding everywhere, and never learns the software is GoHighLevel underneath.

That matters because the client's relationship — and their monthly payment — stays with you, not with the tool. You own the brand they trust, so you own the recurring revenue.

Pricing

The model

How agencies actually make money with it

There are two ways agencies earn here, and the second is the one that scales:

1. Done-for-you service

You set up and run GoHighLevel for a client and charge a monthly retainer. Simple, but your income is capped by your time.

2. Resell it as SaaS (recurring revenue)

With SaaS mode you sell the branded platform itself at your own price. The client pays you monthly to use it; you pay GoHighLevel the wholesale cost. The spread is recurring revenue that keeps coming whether or not you touch the account that month. This is why agencies treat GoHighLevel as a product line, not just a tool.

GoHighLevel agency FAQ

What is a white-label CRM?

A CRM you rebrand and offer to your own customers as if it's your product — your logo, your domain, your pricing. The software underneath is provided by another company (here, GoHighLevel), but your clients only see your brand.

Can you really resell GoHighLevel as your own SaaS?

Yes — that's what SaaS mode is for. You set your own monthly price, connect billing, and clients subscribe to your branded platform. You keep the margin between what you charge and the wholesale cost.

How much does the agency / white-label plan cost?

GoHighLevel starts at $97/month; white-labelling and unlimited sub-accounts come with the higher agency tier, and full SaaS resale with the top plan. See the pricing guide for current numbers.

Is GoHighLevel good for real-estate or other niche agencies?

Yes — agencies serve dentists, real estate, med spas, home services, coaches, and more. Because each client gets a sub-account and you can clone a niche-specific snapshot, the same setup scales across an industry.

Do I need to be technical?

No, but there's a learning curve because the platform does a lot. The free live bootcamp (every weekday) walks you through the first setups click-by-click.
