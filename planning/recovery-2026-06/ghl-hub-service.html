Questions, answered

Service-business FAQ

What's the best CRM for a service-based business?

It depends on where you're losing money. If it's leads, calls, and follow-up, GoHighLevel is built exactly for that and bundles the marketing tools too. If it's scheduling and dispatching crews, a field-service CRM like Workiz or Jobber fits better. Many service businesses run GoHighLevel for the front end (getting and converting customers) and a field tool for operations.

What's the difference between a CRM and field-service software?

A CRM (like GoHighLevel) manages your customers and leads — contacts, calls, texts, follow-up, reviews, marketing. Field-service software (FSM) manages your work in the field — scheduling technicians, dispatching, job costing, invoicing. One grows the top of the business; the other runs the operations.

Can GoHighLevel answer my phone when I can't?

Yes. Missed-call text-back fires an automatic text the moment you miss a call, and Voice AI can actually answer, qualify the caller, and book the appointment 24/7.

Will it help me get more Google reviews?

Yes — you can set it to automatically request a review after each job, which is one of the biggest levers for ranking higher in the local map pack.

Do I need to be technical to set it up?

No, but it does a lot, so there's a setup curve. The free live bootcamp (every weekday) walks you through the first setups click-by-click.
