3 + 4 · “Which are you?” order & the hub links

MEDIUMREC: reorder
Now — routing before the sell
Hero→Bootcamp→Which are you? (hubs)→Why 30 days

People get sent off to a hub before the page has made the 30-day case.

Proposed — sell, then route
Hero→Bootcamp→Why 30 days→Which are you? (hubs)

Routing happens after they’re sold. Plus a small “Start the trial” link on each card so the CTA is never more than one click away.

On #4 (do the hub links steal conversions?): not really — the hubs are your own pages and they also push the trial, so a click there is a more targeted path to the same CTA, not a leak. Fix is the reorder above, not removing the cards.
