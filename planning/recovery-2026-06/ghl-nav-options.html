Navigation design · before the interlinking step

What should live in the menu?

Three options, rendered exactly as they'd look. The decision is mostly SEO: where the hub links live changes which link Google credits. My recommendation is marked.

Caleb's rule that decides this: Google counts only the first link to a page, and the nav sits before your body in the code. Put a hub in the top nav and that weak, generic nav link becomes the "first link" — wasting the credit that should go to your keyword-anchor body link ("GoHighLevel for service businesses →" in the hero cards). So: keep rank-target pages (the hubs & spokes) out of the top nav.
