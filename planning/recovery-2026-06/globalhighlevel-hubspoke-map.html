Hub & Spoke Interlinking · per Caleb (RicketyRoo) + Search Engine Journal

How the pages link — parent, hub, spoke.

Built to the documented hub-and-spoke / parent-child model, applied to your real pages. The homepage is the parent. Each topic is a hub with its spokes. Everything funnels to the commercial pillar (the free-trial blog — your #1 earner). English and Spanish are separate microsites.

01 — THE RULES (pulled, not guessed)

Five mechanics from the docs

1 · UP

Every spoke links back to its hub with a keyword-rich anchor.

2 · DOWN

Hub links down to each of its spokes.

3 · SIBLINGS

Spoke→spoke only when relevant — never link everything to everything.

4 · SILO

Each cluster is its own microsite. Keep it topically clean.

5 · COMMERCIAL

Cross-silo links are fine to the money page, with a transactional anchor.

03 — WHY THIS SHAPE

It matches the money + the docs

The free-trial blog is your real earner (37 affiliate clicks/90d), so it's the commercial pillar every spoke points to with a transactional anchor — exactly what the SEJ rule says to do. Topic hubs build authority for their head terms; spokes feed the hubs. Keeping English and Spanish as separate microsites stops the language-mixing problem you caught, and matches the "each cluster is its own microsite" rule.
