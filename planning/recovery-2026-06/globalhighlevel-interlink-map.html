02 — THE 3 FIXES

What changes in the link graph

+ Add

Homepage → Spanish money pages

The homepage linked to zero Spanish money pages (only the /es/ hub). Now it links straight to Opiniones + Precios — your highest-authority page feeding your #1 lane. Already wired in the prototype.

− Remove

The category leak

Every page (23/24) sends a nav/footer link to the thin /category/agency-platform/. Dropping it from the nav stops bleeding equity into a page we're consolidating away.

↑ Boost

The nearly-orphaned money pages

Payment providers (1 inlink) and Leverage-AI-pricing (2) are barely reachable. Cross-link them from related guides + the homepage hub so they're not stranded.
