The money page's job (off this page): the trial guide stays a focused converter — it links to the affiliate, plus the 2 small "new to GHL? service / agencies" links below its CTA (your routing idea, kept). It is not the funnel; the hubs are.

Decision #1 — two hubs, or three?

The audit found AI / Agent-Studio is your single biggest organic demand (killed pages with 1,862 + 1,162 + 721 impressions). The catch: "AI" is a topic, not a person — so it doesn't fit the "Which are you?" persona question the way Service and Agency do.

How many pillar hubs do we build?
RECOMMENDED

3 pillars — but AI is a topic, not a persona

Keep "Which are you?" as the 2 personas (Service, Agency). Build AI / Automation as a third pillar reached from the "by topic" grid (Decision #2), not from the persona question.

  • Captures the demand you're already getting
  • Keeps "Which are you?" a clean 2-way persona choice
  • Service owners AND agencies both want AI — so it sits across both, as a topic
SIMPLER

2 pillars for now

Service + Agency only. Add the AI hub in a later wave once the two persona silos are ranking.

  • Less to build and interlink right now
  • Leaves your biggest organic demand on the table for now
  • Easy to add later

Decision #2 — the "set up by topic" grid (block 7)

What do we do with the 6-tile grid at the bottom?
RECOMMENDED (pairs with 3 pillars)

Repoint the tiles to the hubs

Keep the visual grid, but the tiles link to your pillars (Service, Agency, AI/Automation) — not 6 random spokes. This becomes the "by topic" home for the AI hub, since AI isn't in "Which are you?".

  • No more scatter — homepage only funnels to hubs
  • Gives the AI pillar a home on the homepage
  • Keeps a familiar grid for scanners
CLEANEST (pairs with 2 pillars)

Remove the grid entirely

Delete it. "Which are you?" is the only path down; the 6 old guides get linked from inside whichever hub owns them.

  • Simplest, tightest homepage
  • Hubs are the single funnel
  • No place to surface an AI pillar on the homepage
They link up: 3 pillars → repoint the grid (it carries the AI pillar). 2 pillars → remove the grid (the two persona cards are enough). Pick #1 and #2 falls out of it.
