Where to actually fight

1

Spanish / LATAM KD 5–9

“gohighlevel” / “go high level” — 9,900/mo each in Mexico, difficulty 5–9. The single best term in the whole sweep. US version of the same word is 165k but KD 49–64 and owned by GoHighLevel. In Spanish it's wide open, and you already have /es/. This is bigger than the entire English service-vs-agency debate.

2

GoHighLevel buyer terms (English) you have a foothold

Pricing 5,400 (you already rank #1-ish), reviews 1,000, “what is gohighlevel” 1,300, “gohighlevel crm” 3,600, “alternative” 390, “starter plan” 1,000. Pure buyer intent = affiliate money. Defend pricing, add the review + comparison pages.

3

AI receptionist / Voice AI growing

“ai receptionist” 5,400, “for small business” 1,000, dental/vertical variants, and GoHighLevel ranks for the conversational-AI space at 550k. Service-business intent, matches GHL's hottest product, and it's the exact content your Bing + ChatGPT channels reward. You already have a draft pillar for this.

4

Small-business CRM KD 50+

Enormous — 192k/mo across “crm for small business” variants. But HubSpot and Zoho own it. Only deep long-tail / vertical angles are realistically winnable. High ceiling, slow climb.

5

Niche easy wedges KD 0

“missed call text back” 1,140 total, “white label crm” 1,110. Easy to rank but small. Good supporting posts that funnel to the trial — not pillars. Agency/white-label is small volume, which is the data answer to your question: the searchable demand is service-side, not agency-side.

The call

Service-led, Spanish-first.

  1. Spanish is the headline opportunity — a /es/ trial homepage + the “gohighlevel” Spanish cluster (KD 5–9, ~10k/mo) is the highest-ROI move on the board. Bigger than anything in English.
  2. English = service-business, on two anchors: the GoHighLevel buyer terms you already rank for (pricing, reviews, alternatives) + the AI-receptionist / Voice-AI cluster that your Bing & AI channels reward.
  3. Agency stays secondary — keep it for your podcast audience, but the volume proves it shouldn't lead.
