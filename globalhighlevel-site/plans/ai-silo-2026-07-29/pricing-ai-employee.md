## How much is a GoHighLevel AI Employee?

Three ways to pay, all per enabled location (sub-account), from [GoHighLevel's AI Product Pricing doc](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006652-ai-product-pricing) as of July 2026:

| Plan | Price | What's included |
|---|---|---|
| **Pay-Per-Use** | $0/month | No monthly fee; every AI action bills at unit rates (below) |
| **AI Employee Growth** | $50/month | 100 Voice AI minutes + 1,000 Conversation AI responses/month; Reviews AI and Content AI unlimited; overages bill at pay-per-use rates |
| **AI Employee Unlimited** | $97/month | Unlimited Voice AI, Conversation AI, Reviews AI, Content AI — subject to fair use; 3× the included Ask AI / AI Studio usage |

The unit rates, when you're paying per use (or past the Growth caps):

- **Voice AI:** a [$0.045/minute Voice Engine rate (effective May 20, 2026)](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006652-ai-product-pricing) plus text-to-speech ($0.015–$0.170/min depending on voice) plus model tokens. The [Voice AI pricing page](/blog/gohighlevel-voice-ai-pricing/) breaks this down per call.
- **Conversation AI:** billed by model tokens (e.g. GPT-5 at $1.25/1M input tokens, $10/1M output).
- **Reviews AI:** [$0.01 per review](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006652-ai-product-pricing).
- **Content AI:** [$0.063 per image and $0.0945 per 1,000 words](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006652-ai-product-pricing).

## Is the $97 unlimited plan actually unlimited?

Mostly yes — with three honest caveats:

1. **Fair use.** GoHighLevel [reserves the right to throttle, limit, or terminate access](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006652-ai-product-pricing) if usage is "excessive, abusive, or negatively affects platform performance." For a normal business answering its own calls, you will not hit this. If you're reselling AI minutes at scale, read it closely.
2. **Phone charges never go away.** [GoHighLevel states it directly](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000001156-highlevel-pricing-guide): "Phone System charges still apply to all phone calls regardless of plan." Telephony (LC Phone or Twilio) is a separate meter on every tier.
3. **Agent Studio isn't in the bundle.** The custom agent builder is [pay-per-use on every plan](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006652-ai-product-pricing) — the $97 tier does not include it. Details on the [AI Agent pricing page](/blog/gohighlevel-ai-agent-pricing/).

Worth knowing: this structure is **newer than most reviews.** Until mid-2026, Voice AI was metered on every tier and "$97 all-in" was genuinely wrong. After the restructure, the $97 tier really does cover unlimited voice usage. If a review tells you voice is always metered, it's describing the old pricing.

## Which AI Employee plan should you pick?

Run the math on your call volume — honestly, because the crossover comes later than most reviews imply:

- **Voice alone, standard voice:** pay-per-use runs about $0.06/minute in engine + speech fees plus tokens. That means 100 minutes costs about $6–7, and pure pay-per-use stays cheaper than the $97 Unlimited tier until roughly **1,500 voice minutes a month**. Versus Growth: $50 covers 100 minutes, then overage at the same rates — Growth plus overage passes $97 at roughly **900 total minutes**.
- **Premium voices move the crossover way up:** an ElevenLabs V3 voice bills $0.215/minute in engine + speech fees, so Unlimited pays for itself past roughly **450 minutes a month**.
- **The bundle is the real reason to pick a tier:** Growth's $50 also covers 1,000 Conversation AI responses, unlimited Reviews AI, and unlimited Content AI — usage that all meters separately on Pay-Per-Use — and Unlimited caps the whole AI bill at $97 no matter what the month looks like.
- **Genuinely tiny or experimental usage:** Pay-Per-Use costs nothing monthly. Good for testing an agent before committing.

One more line item to plan for: the base platform. The add-on requires a GoHighLevel subscription — [$97 Starter, $297 Unlimited, or $497 Agency Pro](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000001156-highlevel-pricing-guide). If you're comparing the whole stack against other platforms, the [full GoHighLevel pricing guide](/blog/gohighlevel-pricing-plans-2026-complete-guide/) itemizes plan-level costs.

It depends on voice choice and volume: with premium ElevenLabs voices past roughly 450 minutes a month, or standard voices past roughly 1,500 minutes, Unlimited beats paying per use — and heavy chat, reviews, or content usage moves the crossover earlier because those meter separately on pay-per-use. No. Phone-system (telephony) charges bill separately on every tier — the $97 covers AI processing, not carrier minutes or phone numbers.
