**GoHighLevel prices AI agents two different ways, and knowing which one you're buying is the whole game: the ready-made agents (Voice AI and Conversation AI) are covered by the AI Employee plans at $50–$97/month, while custom agents built in Agent Studio are [pay-per-use on every plan](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006652-ai-product-pricing) — no subscription includes them, not even the $97 Unlimited tier.** Those are GoHighLevel's published terms as of July 2026. Here's what each path costs and when each makes sense.

## How much do GoHighLevel's built-in AI agents cost?

The agents most businesses mean — the phone-answering Voice AI agent and the chat-handling Conversation AI agent — bill through the AI Employee tiers, per [GoHighLevel's AI Product Pricing doc](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006652-ai-product-pricing):

| Tier | Price | Voice AI agent | Conversation AI agent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pay-Per-Use | $0/mo | $0.045/min engine + TTS + tokens | Token cost per response |
| Growth | $50/mo | 100 minutes/month included | 1,000 responses/month included |
| Unlimited | $97/mo | Unlimited, fair use | Unlimited, fair use |

Phone-system charges apply on every call regardless of tier, and the add-on sits on top of a [GoHighLevel plan at $97–$497/month](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000001156-highlevel-pricing-guide) — the [complete GoHighLevel pricing guide](/blog/gohighlevel-pricing-plans-2026-complete-guide/) itemizes those base plans. The per-minute math is broken down on the [Voice AI pricing page](/blog/gohighlevel-voice-ai-pricing/), and the tier comparison in the [AI Employee plan breakdown](/blog/gohighlevel-ai-employee-pricing/).

## How much does Agent Studio cost?

Agent Studio — the builder for **custom** AI agents — is the exception in the lineup: [not included in any plan, pay-per-use across all of them](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006652-ai-product-pricing). Costs are token-based and vary by the model you pick:

- Language model tokens at API-style rates that [vary by model](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006652-ai-product-pricing) — for scale, the same pricing doc's token tables put a top-tier model like GPT-5 at $1.25 input / $10.00 output per 1M tokens, with mini-tier models at a fraction of that.
- Image generation via DALL-E 3 at [$0.04–$0.12 per image](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006652-ai-product-pricing).

What that means in practice: a custom agent that handles a few hundred text interactions a month costs cents to a few dollars in tokens — the meter only matters at real scale, or with the most expensive models. But because it's usage-billed with no cap, put a number on it: GoHighLevel's [AI usage dashboard](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000007742-ai-usage-dashboard) tracks spend per product, per location.

## Why isn't Agent Studio included in the $97 unlimited plan?

Because "unlimited" scopes to the finished products — [Voice AI, Conversation AI, Reviews AI, and Content AI](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006652-ai-product-pricing) — not the raw model access a custom agent consumes. An unlimited flat fee on arbitrary token usage would be an arbitrage machine (build an agent, resell the tokens), so GoHighLevel meters the builder and caps the promo surface at "Ask AI and AI Studio: 3× usage included" on the Unlimited tier. The naming confusion between AI Studio and Agent Studio is real, and it changes what you pay — the [AI Studio pricing page](/blog/gohighlevel-ai-studio-pricing/) untangles the two.

## Should you use the built-in agents or build custom?

A decision rule that holds up:

- **Answering calls, qualifying leads, booking appointments, handling chat:** use the built-in Voice AI / Conversation AI agents on a Growth or Unlimited plan. They're the productized path — prompt them, connect a calendar, done. Predictable monthly cost.
- **A workflow the built-ins can't express** (multi-step research, custom integrations, image generation, unusual channel logic): that's Agent Studio, priced by the token. Start on pay-per-use, watch the usage dashboard for a month, then decide if the numbers justify scaling it.
- **Agencies reselling agents to clients:** the rebilling machinery ([SaaS mode on the $497 Agency Pro plan](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000001156-highlevel-pricing-guide)) applies markup to AI usage the same way it does to the AI Employee subscription — the margin model works for custom agents too.

## Frequently asked questions

#### How much does a GoHighLevel AI agent cost?

The built-in Voice AI and Conversation AI agents: $50/month (100 minutes + 1,000 responses) or $97/month (unlimited, fair use) per location, plus separate phone charges. Custom agents in Agent Studio: pay-per-use tokens on every plan, typically cents to a few dollars a month at small scale.

#### Is Agent Studio included in the AI Employee plan?

No. Agent Studio is pay-per-use on all plans — including the $97 Unlimited tier. Only the finished AI products (Voice, Conversation, Reviews, Content AI) are covered by the subscription.

#### What's the difference between an AI agent and the AI Employee in GoHighLevel?

"AI Employee" is the billing bundle; the agents are what's in it. Voice AI and Conversation AI are the two agents most people mean, and they're covered by the bundle's tiers. Agents you build yourself in Agent Studio bill separately by usage.

#### How do I keep AI agent costs under control?

Pick the tier that matches your volume (with a standard voice, Growth plus overage passes the $97 Unlimited price at roughly 900 minutes a month — sooner with premium voices or heavy chat usage), keep custom agents on mini-tier models unless quality demands a top-tier one, and check the AI usage dashboard monthly — it itemizes spend per product and location.
